A LITTLE PREPARATION GOES A LONG WAY

Build an order that makes sense.

Think about the meal you want before filling the basket. Then check the small details that can change the final amount.

01

Set your starting point.

Choose your main item before comparing extras. Decide whether you want a sandwich, breakfast or a snack, and whether you plan to eat alone or share.

This gives you a useful baseline. You can see what the rest of the order adds instead of losing track as you browse.

02

Compare like with like.

Read the portion beside each option. A meal combination and a single item include different things, even when the main food looks similar.

For drinks, use the same size in your comparison. Check flavor choices and additions after choosing the cup you actually want.

03

Finish with a full review.

Confirm your selected restaurant and collection method. Check quantities, requested changes and the amount shown at the end of checkout.

For a shared purchase, keep individual requests together. A short list of names and selections can help prevent missing sides or duplicate drinks.

MAINS, SIDES & LITTLE EXTRAS

Leave room for
what you really want.

You can keep an order simple or make a meal of it. Look at each extra as a choice rather than an automatic addition. A side to share might suit your plans better than separate sides for everyone.

Use the final basket to decide. Include every selected item, check any service charges and compare the complete total with your budget.

BREAKFAST & COFFEE

Give your morning a budget.

Decide whether breakfast means one sandwich or a larger order. Begin with the main item, then consider a drink or side.

When comparing options, keep the same drink size in each basket. Check any selected flavors or additions before deciding which breakfast fits your budget.

If you only want a quick bite, compare individual items first. A larger combination is useful when you actually want everything it includes.

MTO & CUSTOMIZATION

Follow the total as you build.

Choose a base you enjoy before adding ingredients. This makes it easier to see which extras improve the meal you had in mind.

Review the price after making changes. Do not assume that every topping, substitution or portion adjustment follows the same pricing rule.

Read the finished item description before checkout. Confirm both additions and removals so the order reflects your choices.

SHARING & GROUP ORDERS

Count people before portions.

Ask everyone for their main item and drink preference first. Then decide which sides, if any, make sense to share.

Check the listed quantity rather than estimating from the product photo. Keep each person’s changes beside their selection when reviewing the basket.

Before paying, count the mains, sides and drinks separately. This simple check helps you catch a missing item or an accidental duplicate.

HELP WITH YOUR MENU COMPARISON

A few things to know.

Are these prices available at every store?+

These are reference examples. Use the selected restaurant’s ordering screen to confirm the price for your item and portion.

What does MTO stand for?+

MTO means Made-To-Order. Review the choices offered for your exact item before selecting ingredients and additions.

Does the price include a complete meal?+

Do not assume a single item includes a side and drink. Read the complete selection to see what is included.

Can I order food from this page?+

This is an independent menu guide. It does not accept payment, send orders or reserve restaurant products.

Why can the final amount change?+

Portion choices, additions, taxes and any applicable service fees can affect the final checkout amount.

How should I use the calorie figures?+

Treat them as references for the listed items. Ingredient and portion changes can alter the figure for your own selection.
