    Sheetz menu with prices: what you'll really pay in 2026

    Most people search for the Sheetz menu with prices for one reason: they want to know what lunch, a coffee run or a late-night snack will cost before they pull in. This guide answers that with real numbers from the board above.

    Sheetz prices at a glance

    Of the 64 items on our board, 7 cost under $3, 36 cost between $3 and $5.99, 14 cost between $6 and $9.99 and 7 cost $10 or more. In other words, more than two thirds of the Sheetz menu sits under $6. The median price is $4.99, the same as a 6-inch Meatball Sub or an MTO chicken sandwich, and the average is $6.20 because a few sharing items like the Bucket Feast pull it up.

    The cheapest and most expensive items

    The cheapest things on the Sheetz menu are the Chocolate Chip Cookie ($0.99), the medium Customizable Coffee ($1.99), the new Apple Fest Fritter (about $2.39), the Shnack Wrap ($2.49) and the Shweetz Gourmet Chocolate Chip Cookie ($2.50). At the top end you'll find the Bucket Feast ($24.78), My Tailgate for 2 – Chicken Combo ($17.25), My Flatz & Shwingz Combo ($13.97) and the Brisket Salad ($13.19). Notice that most of the expensive items are meant for two or more people, so the price per person is often lower than it looks.

    Sheetz salads and smoothies

    Salads are the priciest single-person category. The Low Calorie MTO Salad is about $8.39 and the Made To Order Salad $8.49. The Crispy Chicken and Premium Chicken salads are $10.79 each, and the Brisket Salad is $13.19. You choose the greens, protein, toppings and dressing on the touchscreen. Every base and topping is on the Sheetz Salad Menu.

    Smoothies are a cheaper treat. The Sh'trawberry Fantasy Smoothie is about $4.59, while the Pineapple Aloha and Dazzling Dragon smoothies are $6.99. Flavours rotate with the seasons, so check the Sheetz Smoothie Menu for what's being blended right now.

    Why your Sheetz price may be different

    Sheetz sets prices by market, so a burger in Pennsylvania may not cost the same as one in North Carolina or Michigan. Store location (highway vs. town), local sales tax and limited-time promotions all play a part. Items marked "New" on our board are recent additions with approximate prices. If you want the exact price for your store, the Sheetz app shows it before you order.

    How to pay less at Sheetz

    • Use Sheetz Rewardz. Points on food, drinks and fuel add up quickly if you stop often.
    • Check app deals first. Weekly offers and bundles can beat building the same meal yourself.
    • Swap the drink. A $1.99 coffee instead of a $4.29 seasonal latte saves $2.30 a visit, about $598 a year if you go every weekday.
    • Share the big sides. One $9.99 order of Jalapeño Fryz split three ways is $3.33 each.
    • Order in store. Delivery apps usually add markups and fees on top of menu prices.

    How to read the price board

    Prices on the board are for one standard item before tax. Coffee and latte prices are for a medium (16 oz). Combos include everything named in the combo. Use the price filter to find items under $3, $5, $8 or $12, and the habit calculator to see how small daily buys add up over a year.

    Sheetz menu with prices: FAQ

    How much does a meal at Sheetz cost?

    A typical Sheetz meal of a main, a side and a drink costs about $9 to $13 before tax. For example, an MTO chicken sandwich ($4.99), Mac 'n Cheese Bites ($2.99) and a lemonade ($3.49) come to $11.47.

    What is the average price on the Sheetz menu?

    Across the 64 popular items on this page, the average price is about $6.20 and the median is $4.99. More than two thirds of the menu costs under $6.

    How much is a coffee at Sheetz?

    A medium (16 oz) customizable coffee at Sheetz is about $1.99. An Iced Latte is about $3.49, and seasonal lattes cost around $4.19 to $4.29.

    How much are Sheetz salads?

    Sheetz salads cost about $8.39 for the Low Calorie MTO Salad, $8.49 for the Made To Order Salad, $10.79 for the Crispy or Premium Chicken Salad and $13.19 for the Brisket Salad.

    How much is a Sheetz milkshake?

    Sheetz cheezecake milkshakes cost about $5.49 to $5.79, and the Firewerkz Shake is about $6.29 at typical prices.

    Are Sheetz prices higher on delivery apps?

    Usually, yes. Delivery apps often add a markup plus service and delivery fees, so ordering in store or in the Sheetz app for pickup is normally cheaper.

    Do Sheetz prices include tax?

    No. Menu prices are shown before sales tax. The tax rate depends on your state and local area, which is why the calculators on this page let you set your own rate.

    How often do Sheetz menu prices change?

    Sheetz can change prices at any time, and limited-time items come and go with the seasons. This page lists typical prices last checked in November 2026; the Sheetz app shows the current price at your store.
